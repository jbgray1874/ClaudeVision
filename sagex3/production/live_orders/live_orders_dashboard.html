User guide

Live Orders in the SDI Intelligence portal - for Production, Purchasing and Customer Services. Written 20 Nov 2026, updated 25 Nov (notes typed here, Simon's and Dave's changes).

💬 Ask the guide - e.g. "how do I add a note?", "why is a works order red?", "where is the workbook saved?"

Answers come from this guide only - Claude doesn't see the works orders or the spreadsheets. Please don't type customer or supplier details in the question.

What it is

Production's Live Orders sheet, built from Sage X3: every open works order with its material status, the hours still to do in each work centre, the works orders to raise (WO Suggestions) and the exploded BOM of each order. It follows the same rules as the Access SupplyChain sheet - see How it works. It reads Sage X3; it never changes anything in it. Production's notes (the yellow ✎ columns) are typed on this page and kept by the portal, not in X3.

1. Get today's Live Orders

  1. Choose the Sage X3 environment (top left of the bar): We are SDI (UAT) - where you test - or SDI Mig (test). The badge by the date says which one you're looking at. Each has its own workbooks, named SDI_Live_Orders_UAT_… or …_MIG_…, and its own notes. The choice is remembered on this PC (and used by Estimate → X3 too).
  2. Press ↻ Regenerate (top right). It reads Sage X3 now; the progress shows next to the button. It takes a few minutes: products, BOMs and routes are refreshed overnight, so the day's runs only read the works orders, stock and orders (the very first run in an environment can take 30-60 minutes). Use the page as normal while it runs - it runs on the server, so the tabs, User guide, Workbooks, typing notes or closing the page don't stop it. Only one Regenerate runs at a time, across both environments: if someone else's is running, the button says Busy and comes back when it's done.
  3. When it's done, the line by the button names the new workbook, e.g. SDI_Live_Orders_UAT_2026-11-23_0815.xlsx (UAT = We are SDI, MIG = SDI Mig), with Preview and Download. The views below refresh by themselves.
  4. What X3 sent (by the Done line) lists, for each kind of data, how many rows Sage X3 sent, how many are on the sheet, and why the others were left out (closed, planned, nothing left to make). If no works orders come through, it opens by itself and says why - an empty Live Orders tab always has a reason. It also warns if the stock from X3 doesn't match the part codes (everything would show red).
  5. The workbook is saved in K:\Operations\Reporting\LiveOrders. Each run is a new file with the date and time in its name - nothing is ever overwritten, so anyone can run it at any time.

Red, amber, green - and the shop letters

Material status: GREEN all material in stock · AMBER covered by a PO or works order due in time, or a call-off material · RED short with nothing due in time, or no BOM. Click the line to see which material and why.

Shops: M Metal · J Joinery · A Acrylics - from the work centres on the part's route. Level: Top = the works order for the sales order; Sub = a sub-assembly made for it.

See more on the screen

  • − 100% + (top bar) makes everything smaller or bigger - 70% or 80% shows many more lines at once. It's remembered on this PC.
  • ⛶ Full screen opens Live Orders in its own browser tab, using the whole window (no portal menu). In that tab, F11 hides the browser's own bars too.
  • The Live Orders table fills the screen below the tabs: scroll the page down a little to use all of it. The column headings stay at the top as you scroll the table.

Late POs and chasing suppliers

  1. Open Late POs. It shows the open purchase-order lines past their expected date, most overdue first; choose Late and due this week to see what's coming up too.
  2. WOs waiting are the open works orders that need that part (and how many of them are red) - chase those first.
  3. Pick a supplier and press ✉ Copy chase text: it copies an email listing that supplier's late lines. Paste it into a new email to the supplier, check it, and send it yourself. One email per supplier - if you ever write to several suppliers at once, put them in BCC, never To or CC.

Order lookup - "where's my order?"

Type a sales order, the customer's order reference, an account or a part. Each open line shows the works orders making it, their status (red / amber / green), and their due date - in red if it's after the customer's date. "No works order yet - on WO Suggestions" means Production still needs to raise one.

Data checks before go-live

The Data checks tab lists Sage X3 master data that will cause problems: made parts with no BOM (their works orders show red) or no route (no hours), BOM components that aren't products, route work centres Live Orders doesn't know, products with no description. Click a check to see its list; Export CSV to share it. It updates with every Regenerate, so the list shrinks as things are fixed.

PO Suggestions - what Purchasing should order

  1. Open the PO Suggestions tab. It starts on Order now (and late); choose All to see everything coming up.
  2. Each line is a bought-in part the open works orders are short of. To order = what all the open works orders need, less what's in stock, on open purchase orders and on works orders - the same figure as Shortages.
  3. Needed by is the due date of the first works order the stock and open orders can't cover. Order by = needed by less the part's usual lead time - worked out from its purchase orders in Sage X3 (order date to expected receipt). A lead time marked * is an assumed 14 days: the part has no purchase orders in X3 yet.
  4. Status: Late the works order needing it is already due · Order now the order-by date has come · This week order within 7 days · Later. Parts marked call-off (sheet, powder, packing, pallets, wire, carriage) are called off against an agreement rather than ordered per job.
  5. Last supplier, last price and last ordered come from the part's most recent purchase order in Sage X3 - a starting point, not a rule.
  6. Raise the order in Sage X3 as now. Nothing on this page is sent to X3. At the next Regenerate the new order counts as on order, and the line drops off or shrinks. Export CSV gives you the list to work from.

Check before ordering: minimum order quantities, pack sizes and price breaks aren't in the suggestion yet, and a sub-assembly made in house can already be on a works order.

3. Production's notes - type them here

  1. On Live Orders, the yellow ✎ columns are yours: Comments, Prog, Tube req, Material (blank for your own material notes - the reason a line is red or amber is in the drop-down when you click the line) and Notes.
  2. Click a cell, type, and press Enter to save (Shift+Enter for a new line, Esc to cancel). Clicking away also saves. The first time, it asks your name - once per PC.
  3. Everyone sees the note straight away (the page checks for new notes every 30 seconds). Hover over a note to see who wrote it and when. To clear one, delete the text and press Enter.
  4. Notes stay with the works order: each Regenerate puts them in the new workbook too, so nothing is copied over on Monday. Each environment has its own notes.

Notes can only be typed on the latest run - an older one opened with View in the dashboard shows them read-only. Every change is logged (who, when, old and new text).

4. The portal or the spreadsheet?

  • The portal is the daily working tool. Everyone sees the same live page; notes typed here are shared at once, with names and times. Use it to plan, filter, look at an order's BOM, and for Purchasing's Shortages.
  • The spreadsheet is the saved record. Each Regenerate saves a dated copy in K:\Operations\Reporting\LiveOrders, with the notes as they were. Use it to print (A3, one page wide), to send, to sort or total your own way in Excel, and to look back at an earlier run.
  • Notes typed in the newest spreadsheet (opened from the folder, not a downloaded copy) still count: they come into the portal at the next Regenerate. If the same cell was changed in both, the later change wins. Notes typed in an older spreadsheet or a download are not read.
  • The spreadsheet isn't shared live - two people can't type in it at once. That's why notes are typed in the portal.

5. Look back

  • 📂 Workbooks lists every spreadsheet in the folder: sort newest / oldest first or by name, search, and Preview any of them here, tab by tab, without opening Excel.
  • View in the dashboard shows an older run in all the views; the header then says "viewing …" - press back to latest to return.

If something isn't right

The line next to Regenerate says what went wrong, in plain words. A number that doesn't look right: check it in Sage X3, then tell James Gray (with the works-order number). The rules are on How it works.

How Live Orders works it out

The logic reverse-engineered from Access SupplyChain and Production's sheet, reconciled to Access on 16 Nov 2026, applied to live Sage X3 data. The workbook's "How it works" tab says the same.

Workbooks

Every Live Orders spreadsheet in Production's folder. Each generation is saved with its date and time, never overwritten.

Copy folder path

To edit, open the workbook from the folder (Copy path, then paste in Excel's Open box or File Explorer) - a downloaded copy doesn't go back to the folder. Notes are typed on the Live Orders tab (shared by everyone); notes typed in the newest workbook also carry forward to the next generation.
