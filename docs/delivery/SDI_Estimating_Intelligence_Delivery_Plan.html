01Key dependencies

Dependency 1 — Estimating capacity

The acceptance gates take estimator review as their evidence, so the plan is paced around the validation the estimating team can accommodate — not the other way round. That team is carrying a very large volume of live customer work, which is exactly why the plan has to be built on what is realistic rather than on what would suit the launch date.

The position has improved this week, and that is down to estimating. James Ryan has increased testing and re-affirmed his support, reviewing four estimates in short order, and will look at the two Tesco jobs sent to engineering estimations by Nick Garrish. Tim has been genuinely busy this week and has still picked up both live estimates and started on them. Four reviewed and two under way against that workload is good going.

Next step: agree together how much validation time can realistically be found alongside the live queue, and report reviews completed each week, so the plan reflects what is achievable.

Dependency 2 — There is no server fit to host this

SDI-APP01 (10.0.0.5) cannot take this workload. It is a 32 GB host already carrying the SOLIDWORKS PDM estate (Archive service, ConisioDb, the PDM SQL instance and the SolidNetWork licence manager), the TRUMPF stack (TRUMPFSQL2 and its brokers) and the SDI Intelligence portal. Two SQL Server instances on 32 GB is tight before anything is added.

The estimating engine is not a light addition. It drives SOLIDWORKS and Excel through COM, which requires a licensed interactive desktop session — not a Windows service. A SOLIDWORKS session on a real assembly is several gigabytes and CPU-hungry. Co-locating that with the PDM Archive service means a hung Excel or a SOLIDWORKS crash becomes a PDM outage rather than a failed estimate.

Consequence for the date: lead times have not yet been established, but between resourcing, cost approval and hardware availability it is unlikely that a permanent server can be ready by 7 September. The launch therefore proceeds on an interim host (tasks 29–30), with the permanent AI host specified, quoted, approved and procured in parallel and delivered once available. That sequencing is deliberate and is what keeps the date.

03What is proven today

Stated as evidence rather than activity, per the directive.

End-to-end workflow — proven 5 August 2026

  • A live enquiry pack was selected from the CAD share through the web page, run at a stated order quantity, and a complete deliverable set filed to the estimating share: costed workbook, client quote, decision report, summary JSON and the full run console.
  • Output is written to \\sdi-dc01\shareddata$\Shared\Estimating\Completed\AI Estimating\AISheets\<Client>\<Drawing> with a dated sub-folder per run, so re-running a drawing can never overwrite an earlier estimate.
  • Finished estimates are browsable and readable from the page itself — reports open in a tab, the workbook downloads to Excel.

Live estimating with the team

  • Eight jobs run AI-first and passed to estimating: 12422-24 End Cap and 113509 Ladder Rack Comms Bar for Boots with Tim; four M&S jobs with James Ryan; two Tesco jobs being priced for Nick — the first work routed through an account manager rather than estimating directly.
  • Parity figures are deliberately not quoted: a parity number means nothing until an estimator has reviewed the sheet and named the differences. That review is the item running behind.
  • TTi packs and hand-drawn packs have not been run. The agreed spread is M&S, Boots, Tesco and TTi; three of four are now represented.

Recorded demonstration

12422-24 End Cap · Boots · 10 off, recorded 5 August — the full loop from an empty form to a filed estimate. If the player is empty this report is not filed in its usual place; the video can always be opened from the share at \\sdi-dc01\shareddata$\...\AISheets\Boots\12422-24\2026-08-05 13-12-03.mp4. The video is not embedded — this report stays a few tens of kilobytes and nothing downloads until play is pressed.

Engine capability

  • Reads drawing packs as a job: PDF, DXF and SOLIDWORKS native models, with a documented source precedence so a measured value is never overwritten by an inferred one.
  • Assembly hierarchy is extracted from the native models and applied to costing, so an operation charged on a parent is not charged again on its child.
  • Order quantity is honoured end to end — batch economics, setup recovery and the workbook order quantity all move together and are checked by an invariant.
  • Every run reports its own gaps: unread files, missing extracts, provisional prices and unverified findings are printed rather than absorbed.

Known gaps, stated plainly

  • DWG files are not read. The ODA File Converter is not deployed, so a later revision sitting in a pack as a DWG is invisible to the engine. Observed on a live pack this week.
  • No user authentication on the portal. Anyone who can reach the port can browse the CAD vault with the service's rights. This is a go-live gate, not a nice-to-have.
  • The SOLIDWORKS extract is a separate manual step today. Without it, the assembly hierarchy does not apply and the estimate is quietly weaker than it looks.

05Infrastructure assessment and required spend

All AI work currently runs on three developer laptops. That is the single largest operational risk in the programme after validation throughput: the production estimating capability, its output history and its learning corpus sit on machines that are not backed up as servers, not resilient and not available to anyone else.

Required purchases

  • Dedicated AI / estimating host, kept separate from the PDM estate. Specification under task 26, quotations task 27, capital approval task 28.
  • Memory uplift for SDI-APP01 — 32 GB is under-provisioned for the PDM and TRUMPF estate it already runs, irrespective of this programme.
  • SOLIDWORKS seat and Office licence for the runner account. Without both, the host is provisioned and unusable — COM automation cannot run headless.
  • No separate API licence is required. The engine binds SldWorks.Application — the COM API included with any SOLIDWORKS seat, at no additional per-quarter charge. The key-licensed Document Manager API serves the Design Automation workstream and is not a substitute: it cannot carry estimating, so the seat and the Office licence must both be in the specification.
  • Domain service account SDI\svc-sdiintel with read on the CAD share, modify on AISheets, and Allow log on locally. Requested 5 August.
Sequencing decision

Lead times are not yet established, but between resourcing, cost approval and hardware availability a permanent server is unlikely to be ready by 7 September. Rather than couple the launch to procurement, the plan launches on an interim host and moves to the permanent AI host once available, during or after hypercare. This protects the date without shipping onto a platform that puts PDM at risk.

08Weekly delivery dashboard

Per the directive's cadence, reported every Friday and reviewed weekly with Charlotte. The dashboard answers one question: are we on track to launch v1 on the committed date?

  • Committed launch date and overall RAG.
  • Milestones due last week — delivered, missed or carried, with reason and recovery owner.
  • Milestones due next week and the evidence required to close each one.
  • Jobs shipped, returned, validated, accepted and rejected — the leading indicator for this programme.
  • Average estimator review time and manual time avoided, against the benefit baseline.
  • Critical and major defects: open, age, owner, planned closure.
  • Blockers, decisions required, and any proposed scope change with its launch impact.
  • New ideas parked outside v1.
