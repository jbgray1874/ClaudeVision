## What to look for on this pack that 02 could not test

- **Real weights.** 02's zero weights switched every weight check off. Here the frame states 398.43 g
  and the rectangular blank at 1.2 mm weighs 942 g, 2.4× the part — the gap is the window, the
  aperture and the empty corners beside the base tab, all of which the DXF measures. The material must
  be costed by nest off the blank as on 02, with the stated weight as a check only. A question saying
  "the blank weighs more than its part — confirm the gauge" would be the check misreading a cut-out
  for a thinner sheet: 1.2 mm is printed, modelled and in the DXF's name. The GA's 402.09 g must not be
  reconciled against anything.
- **The GA's DXF is a sheet, not a part.** `9598-03-GA.dxf` holds the drawing border (BORDER_-_FORMAT),
  eleven dimensions and the title-block text, extents 420 × 297. It must not become a flat pattern, a
  blank or a cut file for the GA or for any part (a GA is never cut; a DXF is read only where it adds —
  D-316). If a 420 × 297 steel blank appears anywhere, that is what happened.
- **The frame's DXF has stray arcs.** Beside the five closed loops (outer profile, window, aperture,
  two Ø5 holes) it carries four overlapping partial arcs at the window's lower corners and the holes.
  On 02 the same export left the outline "not fully closed", so the pierce count was a floor and the
  model's five pierces displaced the DXF's two. Expect the same here: pierces 5 from the model, the
  DXF's cut length used as measured. If the book asks "confirm the drawing shows every cut-out", the
  answer is yes: window, aperture, two holes.
- **Folds.** Two bend lines on the DXF (y = −251.9 and −100.6), two callouts on the sheet (UP 90° R 1,
  DOWN 180° R 0.5), two model bend features. On 02 the run read the callouts as 3 where every text
  layer prints 2; D-402 made PyMuPDF the deciding layer. If "callouts read 3" appears again, the
  count must now name its layer (D-403, pending) — note it and move on; the charge is 2 either way.
- **Colour.** RAL 9010 on the P.Coat row and in the powder line; nothing may carry 7021 from the sister job.
- **The deburr row** (£1.69 on 02) stands on the border's "ALWAYS REMOVE BURRS AND SHARP CORNERS",
  which the vision read reports as a note on the sheet. Tony's call whether M&S steel always carries
  it; pending D-403 the row should say where it came from.
- **The product** must resolve from "9598-03" typed on the portal to 9598-03-GA with 01M, 02G and
  FIXING1270 under it, nothing set aside (D-395, D-398).
