## Finish

Powder matt RAL9005, 80–120 µm, on the steel parents only: frame 201, eight pockets, four rails,
seven hooks, top rack, trough. MDF is wet spray. MFC is the oak décor. China Q195/SPCC/304/6063
boilerplate does not override a part's own material box.

## UPC

Code is `//`, so a text extract drops it. It is on the kit and again on each pocket, rail and
hook GA: **1 + 8 + 4 + 7 = 20**. Words only. Do not turn `//` into a cut part, and do not drop it.

## What fails the book

- A second product (03-GA, or any other GA, costed on top of 02-GA).
- Tabs at 32, shelves at 2, pockets at 1, hooks at 1, rails at 1.
- The two pan-head screws merged.
- Tube, hook wire, riser wire or mesh lasercut as sheet.
- 263 kg rail, 26 kg bracket, or a 0 kg tab used as mass.
- Powder on MDF or MFC, or powder on the RAW members as well as the parent.
- Wet spray missing on the base and the plate top.
- Lazy susan, glides, inserts, nutserts, pips or the nine ticket strips missing.
- One hand only of 04-02M, 07-1-02M or 07-2-02M.
- A GA DXF becoming a cut part.

## Breaks

No quantity on the drawing, so the six breaks are the run. At 1 off the money is press and TIG
set-up, a powder minimum, and delivery of one 52 kg team-lift unit; that unit is not comparable
with 500 off. Real weld is the two tube frames (TIG and dress), the 16 tabs, the four rails and the
seven hooks. The pockets are tab-and-slot plus bought mesh.
