## The right book

- **The joints are notes, not symbols.** Sheet 2 says WELD & DRESS on the frame and TAC WELD UNDERSIDE where the base meets it. Both belong to `101`. The three steel leaves are RAW and carry neither a weld nor a coat. This pack has no "all welds to be TIG" line, so a TIG suffix on the weld row would be invented.
- **Powder on `101` only.** MATCH C31 ANODISED is the colour the powder is matched to; it is not an anodise operation and must not raise one.
- **The weights are the check, and they close.** 002 at 811.7 × 19.1 × 1.2 mm is 146 g. 003 at 204.6 × 7.2 × 1.2 mm is 14 g. 001 at 5 mm is a rim, 105 g; a solid 219 × 60 plate would be about 516 g. 101 is 105 + 146 + 14 + 14 = 279 g. The GA is 279 + 151 + 151 + 10 = 591 g.
- **Quantities.** 003 and 004 are 2 each, not 1.
- **Material charged on four lines:** 001, 002, 003, 004. Nothing on the GA, 101 or 005.

## What will go wrong if the last job's habits repeat

- **One PDF has to be split into seven sheets**, each costed part traced to its own.
- **The DXF for 002 shows only the two notches** while the sheet calls eight bends: expect 8 from the callouts, with the source named; the model is not in the pack.
- **005 must not be lasered**, priced or inferred a material: a free-issue line at £0 with the reason.
- **001 is a rim.** The net-area reader took the hole for the blank and the weight check weighed the envelope (both fixed in D-396 before the first run). Expect no gauge question on 001 and material from the 5 mm plate at the rim's nest.
- **"MATCH C31 ANODISED"** in the colour field: any anodising operation or plating question is the colour read as a process.
- **Two tiny strips (003)** nest in the hundreds per sheet: a laser row per component, pence of material.
