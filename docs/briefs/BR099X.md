for generic use". Footprint 500 × 500 mm. One shopping face of full-width shelves, two side faces of
mirrored shelves, a rear graphic panel; a central top-hat core.

## The right book

- **Steel, folded and powder coated:** six slotted corner posts (lasered slots, folded), the top-hat
  spine, top cap (with its slot), three headers, rear graphic panel, base plinth, two graphic holders,
  fifteen shelf trays (five front, ten side, the side ones handed) each with two hooked brackets —
  welded or riveted on, one route chosen and named.
- **One neutral powder coat** over the whole unit.
- **Assembly and pack:** flat-packed for SEC Castleford, "minimal on-site assembly"; packaging and
  delivery to SEC as lines.
- **Not in it:** graphics, card inserts and mag-graphics ("All display aids are being managed
  separately"); lighting and digital (out of scope); development, drawings, prototypes, test rigs,
  installation guides, O&M — the brief asks for these as separate costings, outside the book.

## What will go wrong, and what to look for

- **The podium.** Slide 3's SOTF Podium (501 × 451, Beauty/Photo/Baby variants) is not this unit.
  Any line sized from it, or a footprint of 501 × 451, is the wrong page in the pack.
- **The height and the gauges are assumptions.** The book must say so on the unit and in the banner;
  a figure that reads as checked is wrong.
- **Shelf brackets as separate parts.** The brief says the hooked brackets are welded or riveted to
  the tray, not separate; two per shelf, thirty in all, and the fixing is a route, not a bought-in.
- **Handed side shelves.** Ten side shelves, five of each hand — not ten of one part, not twenty.
- **Graphics priced.** Card and mag-graphics are Boots' own; a print line is out of scope.
- **Quantity basis.** The brief prices at 200; the book's unit cost at 1 off is a prototype figure,
  setup-dominated.
