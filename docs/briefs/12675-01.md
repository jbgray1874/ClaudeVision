## The two designs

coated RAL 7021 black grey. Footprint 600 × 400 with R25 corners, body 1250 tall on adjustable feet
(16.5), 1580 to the top of the stacked bags. Two stacks of ten bags side by side, a central solid
divider so the handles cannot tangle, open front windows down each face (the section shows the bag
stack resting in a V), four adjustable feet. Estimated topple angle 17.82°; "weight can be added to
base if required". Bag pitch 100, estimated. About 37 kg of 2 mm steel: roughly two sides 400 × 1250,
front and back 600 × 1250 with the windows cut out, a 600 × 400 base and a 400 × 1250 divider —
around 3 m² gross before the windows.

**V2 — Two-tier tree** (12675-01-02). A 5 mm brushed stainless steel base 600 × 400 sitting on an
18 mm black MFMDF board with feet (25 mm), a 40 × 20 stainless flat-sided oval tube post 1500 tall,
capped and finished on top, and two tiers of 5 mm lasered stainless arms (at 700 and 1500) each
holding six bags on 160 pitch, 930 across the arms, 1005.4 with the bags. Twelve bags. Detail A: the
arms notch over the post (10 / 21 mm). Estimated topple angle 19.14°. About 17 kg: the stainless base
is 9.4 kg of it.

## What will go wrong, and what to look for

- **The three DXFs are sheets, not flats.** Border, dimension and note layers with the drawing views
  as blocks (Drawing View1…, Section View A-A, SW_TABLEANNOTATION). If a blank of 5880 × 4158, 6720 ×
  4752 or 8400 × 5940 appears, or any part named after a DXF stem, a sheet export was read as a part.
  Nothing in them is a flat pattern (D-316: a GA is never cut; a DXF is read only where it adds).
- **The weights must not price anything.** 67.44 kg and 34.82 kg include the bags; 83.86 kg is an
  earlier concept. A steel line "costed from the stated weight" at 67 kg would be twice the stand.
- **"SEE PART DRAWINGS" is a pointer, not a material** (D-404). It must not reach a Material column
  or the quotation. The material of each design comes from the brief and the sheet notes.
- **The legend** (TIG, powder specification, 304 stainless, 6063 aluminium, timber products) says how,
  not that. V1 is powder coated because its own note says so; V2 is brushed stainless because its own
  note says so; neither gets the other's finish, and no aluminium or timber appears.
- **Bags are not parts.** "20 x CUSTOMER BAGS", the bag views (556.5 × 186.5 × 662) and the 1.5 kg
  are the customer's goods. Any line for a bag is wrong.
- **ESTIMATED / TBC / TO BE DETERMINED** dimensions (bag pitch, V1 height, bag dims) are assumptions
  the book must name, not facts it may state.
- **V2's stock:** a 40 × 20 flat-sided oval stainless tube may have no SDI Live row; expect a research
  rung with its source and date, or a hold at the £/kg rate, said on the sheet. 5 mm stainless sheet
  and 18 mm MFMDF should price from stock.
- **The portal product:** type the drawing number of the design being run (12675-01-GA for V1,
  12675-01-02 for V2), not 12675-01 alone, so the two concepts never collapse into one line.

## First run — 7 Oct 17:52 book on 5a7d6d4, the DRAWINGS method at 10 off (£31.17 a unit) — not an estimate

Run as an engine job over the whole folder (four PDFs, three DXFs, the SolidWorks models), which
is the wrong method for this pack. With no parts list and no part sheets the readers minted a part
from each design-intent drawing number and costed it: `12675-01-02` as a "20 mm stainless" sheet
part with a 40 × 20 blank (the oval tube's section read as a blank and its 20 as the gauge), 1.5 kg
(the bag's estimated weight), lasered over 14 m of drawing ink, folded off 59 dashed lines and
powder coated over 0.0016 m²; `12675-01-BLOCK` as a 2 mm part, 30 × 30, 83,860 g (the 25/09
concept's weight with the bags), unpriced, folded off 229 dashed lines. The stacking GA was not a
line at all. The SolidWorks extract was refused, two DXFs were rightly rejected as drawings, three
checks failed, and the report still said the pack read cleanly. Nothing in £31.17 is a stand: no
tube length, no wire, no 5 mm plate, no board, no feet, no weld. Material £3.59, labour £24.57 of
four default rows, margin nil. James Gray's verdict: do not hand this book to an estimator as a
starting price; send the pack back for a production drawing.

Fixed as D-406 (generic): a sheet that says it is design intent takes its own page role and mints
no part; a drawings run over a pack with nothing else stops with the reason and names the method
that answers it; a size that names a section (40 × 20 tube, 30 × 30 × 2 SHS) is never a blank or a
gauge; a weight the sheet qualifies as something else's (ESTIMATED BAG WEIGHT) is never the part's;
and the report cannot call a pack clean beside a failed check, a refused extract or a design-intent
sheet.
