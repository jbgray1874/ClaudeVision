## The right book

- **One weld and its dress, on `12696-01-101` only.** The evidence is the symbols on sheet 2. Nothing else is welded: not the GA, not the brackets, not the strip, not the fasteners.
- **Powder on `101` only**, RAL9007, over the two brackets' blanks. The brackets are RAW and are coated as the weldment. The GA refers to the sub-assembly and carries no coat of its own. The PETG and the fasteners are never coated.
- **The two brackets lasered and folded, raw**: 01M one bend, 02M three bends and a hole. No DXF exists; the bends are the sheet callouts and the model's bend features.
- **The PETG strip is a drawn plastic part with its own model**: cut and bent on the acrylic side, scraped edges, not a Windmill strip, not a steel laser part, not powder coated.
- **Material charged on five lines**: 01M, 02M, 01A, the thumbscrew, the hank bush. Nothing on the GA or the weldment, whose material is their children's.

## What will go wrong on the current engine, and what to look for

- **No DXF.** Every fold is on the PDF and in the model. A reader that counts only DXF bend lines finds none. Expect: 01M 1 fold, 02M 3 folds, 01A 3 bends, each from the sheet callouts or the model, with the source named on the line.
- **No sheet states a thickness.** The weights are the only check. Whatever gauge the engine settles on, the material mass across the five lines must land near 0.11 kg, and the decision that chose the gauge must be on the review list, not silent.
- **The powder, chrome and zinc paragraph is on every sheet**, including the raw brackets and the PETG. The title block is the finish; the paragraph is not. Any coat on 01M, 02M, 01A or the GA is the legend leaking.
- **"ALL WELDS TO BE TIG UNLESS STATED" is the same pack note**, on the plastic page as well. Any weld anywhere but 101 is the legend leaking.
- **`FIXING 297` has a space in it; the thumbscrew code has a hyphen and spaces.** Each must be one line. Two lines for either is the spelling split that doubled a screw on 12173.
- **`01A` is PETG with an "A" suffix.** The non-metal reader will tag its page; the "-A" convention and its own model must keep it a part we make, on the plastic-sheet block, not a bought-in line and not a steel nest.
- **The GA's "REFER TO SUB ASSEMBLY"** must not be read as a material or a finish of its own.
