## Three of them are worth a sentence more

**The brushing.** You told us 40 minutes was "given by production for one unit", and we had
it recorded as once for the whole consignment — at six off that understated the operation
six-fold. It is per unit now. It is also on a row of its own rather than pooled with
deburring and bench work, which is why it was still showing the wrong rate after we had the
figure right.

**The two packs.** You were asked whether you would rather see them split and said two
separate operations. They are two rows. One thing to rule: **each row takes the template's
own PACM set-up, so the split books two set-ups** — one for packing out, one for packing
back. If the bench treats those as one visit, say so and the second comes off.

**The tube bend.** We did not simply delete it when you said it was not required. The
drawing's own text mentions a bend, and an engine that removes charged work because one
person disagrees with a drawing stops describing anything. So it charged it, flagged it,
and waited for a ruling — which is what your answer now is. It is recorded against this job
and it governs this job only.
