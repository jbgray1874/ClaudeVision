◆ This week's specific ask — delivered

Amend a sheet, upload it, get a new quote Built & Working

Owner · James Gray  ·  Requested by · Dave

The request was for “an area to the intelligence tool to ensure when they manually modify a prepopulated sheet, they have an area to reupload and regenerate a new quote ready for next week.” That is built, tested and on the portal.

  • The estimator's own figure is the one that goes out. The sheet is read for Sell Price in preference to Unit Cost, so a margin they set is what the customer sees.
  • Hours or days later is fine. They re-enter units, drawing number and client; nothing needs re-running and no drawings are needed.
  • The full quotation is preserved — material, finish, operations and the GA image come from the original run, with only the money and those three fields overlaid.
  • Their sheet is kept as the record, filed as <job>_MANUAL_OVERRIDE.xlsx alongside the quote on the AI Estimating share.
  • One operational note for training: the workbook must be opened and saved in Excel first. The totals are formulas, and Excel only stores the computed values on save. If it has not been, the tool says so rather than producing a blank quote.
Why this matters for Week One. The 24–28 August plan has estimators working from pre-populated sheets and sending their final versions back for quoting. That loop now exists as a page rather than a developer task, which is what makes the week runnable.

£ New blocker — SOLIDWORKS licences have expired

Model reading is stopped until seats are in place Spend Decision

Owner · James Gray  ·  Plan task 26 — due 19 August, Not Started

The introductory SOLIDWORKS subscriptions used for the last six weeks (one month free plus a two-week extension) have expired, and SOLIDWORKS will not extend them further. Task 26 in the plan — “SOLIDWORKS COM API licence + seat + Office for the runner” — was due today and is not started.

What this stops. The engine reads .sldprt and .sldasm through the native SOLIDWORKS COM API, which is what gives a part its true material, gauge, flat pattern and weldment cut list. Without a seat it falls back to reading PDFs, which is materially less accurate, and the run says so rather than pretending otherwise. Model-dependent development and testing is paused — including the tube/weldment reader, which is written and waiting on a seat to be proven.

Cost, and the offsetting case. SOLIDWORKS Design Standard is £240 per user per month. Two seats are needed now (James, Yogesh) at £480 a month, with a third for the server when it is commissioned. A separate case has been put to the MD and FD covering these alongside the server, and offsetting them against approximately £50,000 over three years of PDM upgrade that SDI Technical Intelligence can avoid.

◈ Model Engine — accuracy and honesty work

What landed since the last update Landed

Owner · James Gray
  • Incomplete packs are now stated everywhere the estimator looks. A BOM naming drawings the pack does not contain is named on the Estimate sheet, the AI Provenance tab, the Decision Report and the job report — and deliberately not on the customer's quotation, where scope is the estimator's to word. Proven on the Dyson 10575-02 pack, which is missing four welded assemblies.
  • New materials costed rather than silently zeroed — Dibond and composite panel recognised, routed correctly (CNC router, not laser, which would melt it) and priced; a standard pallet given a stable price instead of a figure that changed every run.
  • A phantom part removed. The draughtsman named in a title block was being costed as an 18mm MDF panel at £28 on a job with no MDF in it. A person is no longer a part.
  • Provenance made trustworthy. The tab reported “0 HIGH / 22 LOW” on a job it had read well, because it scored each part by its weakest field — almost always the price, which an estimator has not yet entered. Reading and pricing are now reported separately.
  • Old jobs pick up engine improvements automatically. Previously a fix only reached a job whose models had changed since — so 2019 and 2025 packs kept using a stale read indefinitely.
  • Still open — the weldment/tube reader. Written and tested offline; needs a SOLIDWORKS seat and a pack with models to prove. Blocked on the licence, not on engineering.
