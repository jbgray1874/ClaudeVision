HRInventry Anywhere

1. What it does

Staff clock in and out on BrightHR Blip. InVentry is the reception system, and its list of who is on site is the fire roll call. On its own InVentry does not know about Blip clockings. This app:

  • shows who is clocked in on BrightHR right now;
  • updates InVentry to match: signs in people who have clocked in, and signs out people the app signed in once they clock out;
  • runs the fire roll call, with tick boxes every marshal shares;
  • lists data problems to tidy in InVentry, and forgotten clock-outs to correct in BrightHR.

It never changes BrightHR: it only reads it. In InVentry it only signs existing staff in and out. It never adds, edits or deletes anyone's record, and never touches visitors or contractors.

2. Getting started

  1. On your phone or laptop, open sdiintelligence-sdidisplays.msappproxy.net/app. It works over mobile data or any Wi-Fi; no VPN is needed.
  2. Sign in with your usual SDI Microsoft account, not an admin account.
  3. Tap Open HRInventry Anywhere →.
  4. On a phone, choose Add to Home Screen from the browser menu, so it opens like an app.

Who can use it

Only people on the app's named list, because it shows who is in the building and changes the fire roll call. If you see Not on the list, ask James Gray to add you.

In the office, the same check and update are also on the intranet portal at http://10.0.0.5:8071 → SDI People Data Intelligence · BrightHR.

3. Check and Update

Check who's clocked in

Asks BrightHR about every current employee and lists who is clocked in, with the time each person clocked in. It changes nothing anywhere. It takes 1 to 4 minutes. You can leave the page; it carries on, and the result is waiting when you come back.

Update InVentry

Does a fresh check, then brings InVentry into line with it. It asks you to confirm first, because it changes the fire roll call. When it finishes it shows:

  • Signed in and Signed out: who it changed;
  • Not matched: people it could not find in InVentry, so did not update (see Data issues);
  • InVentry now shows on site: the full list, read back from InVentry itself.

Only one check or update runs at a time. If someone else's is running, you are told whose, and when it started.

4. The tabs

Latest

The most recent check or update, whoever ran it, with when it ran. Anyone clocked in for more than 20 hours is marked in orange.

Group by manager puts the clocked-in list under each person's manager, one folded section per manager with how many people are in it. Tap a manager to open or close their team. Anyone without a manager is together at the end, under No manager listed. Show as one list goes back. The phone remembers which you chose, on both tabs.

Where the managers come from

BrightHR does not share who manages whom with other systems, so managers come from the HR managers list: a spreadsheet with every current employee and a yellow Manager column. HR types each person's manager there; a filter by department fills a whole team at once. Changes show after the next Check who's clocked in. New starters appear under No manager listed until they are added: ask James to refresh the list, which keeps everything already typed.

Roll call

Everyone who should be in the building, each with a tick box. It combines everyone InVentry shows on site (including people signed in at reception) with everyone clocked in on BrightHR at the last check. Each name says where it comes from, for example clocked in 08:05 · not on InVentry.

  • Ticks are shared: every phone with the app open sees every tick within a few seconds, with who ticked and when.
  • Show only people not yet accounted for narrows the list to who is still missing.
  • Group by manager folds the list by BrightHR manager, each section saying how many of that manager's team are accounted for (for example 2 of 5 accounted for). People signed in at reception who are BrightHR staff go under their manager too. Sections you open stay open as the list refreshes. Visitors, and anyone without a manager in the HR managers list, are under No manager listed.
  • Start a new roll call clears every tick on every phone.
  • Forgotten clock-outs are left out: they are not in the building.

Data issues

Press Run the data check. In under a minute it compares every BrightHR employee with InVentry's staff records, changes nothing, and lists:

SectionWhat to do
Duplicate InVentry recordsOne person on several records. The app uses the copy marked (used). Merge or remove the others, as reception may sign in on them.
One email or ID shared by different peopleGive each person their own email or ID in InVentry. Until then they are matched by full name only.
BrightHR staff with no InVentry matchThese people are never signed in to InVentry, so they are missing from InVentry's list when on site. Correct their name or email in InVentry.
Accepted with a name differenceMatched, but the names differ (Elizabeth / Liz). Check they are the same person.
InVentry records with no current BrightHR employeeUsually leavers or old copies. The app never touches them; remove them if they are no longer needed.

Run it again after tidying to see the list shrink.

Forgotten clock-outs

People still clocked in on BrightHR more than 20 hours later, from the latest check. Their BrightHR hours are wrong until the clocking is closed, in BrightHR, with the time they actually left. They are not counted as on site.

History

The latest checks and updates: when, who ran them (this app, the intranet page, or the scheduled run), and who was signed in or out.

5. In a fire evacuation

Visitors and contractors

This roll call is staff only. Take the visitor and contractor list from reception as usual.

  1. Open the app and tap Roll call. The list appears in seconds; do not wait for a new check.
  2. One person, the lead marshal, taps Start a new roll call so ticks from a previous drill are cleared. Everyone else just opens the Roll call.
  3. Tick each person as they are accounted for. Every marshal's phone shows the same ticks.
  4. Turn on Show only people not yet accounted for. That list is who is still missing.
  5. Give the missing names to the fire service. Each name shows where it came from, which helps judge whether the person is really in: for example, clocked in on BrightHR, or signed in at reception.

The panel says when BrightHR was last checked. Names from InVentry are always current. Names from BrightHR are as of that check, so someone who clocked out after it may still be listed.

If the signal drops, keep ticking: ticks are kept on your phone and sent when it returns.

Practise with a drill: start a new roll call, tick a few names on two phones, and check both phones agree.

7. How it decides

  • Clocked in on Blip, not signed in on InVentry: signed in.
  • Clocked out on Blip, signed in by the app: signed out.
  • Signed in at reception: never signed out by the app; reception signs them out as usual.
  • Matching: by ID, then email (only if the name also agrees), then full name (only if exactly one record has it). If it is not sure, it does nothing and lists the person as not matched.
  • Forgotten clock-outs: a clock-in open for more than 20 hours is ignored.
  • Bad data: it changes nothing if BrightHR's answer is more than 15 minutes old, if more than 2% of the BrightHR lookups failed, or if BrightHR's staff list cannot be read.
  • Every change in InVentry is marked BRIGHTHR SYNC, so it can be told apart from reception's.

8. Hours and pay

Use BrightHR for hours

InVentry stamps each sign-in and sign-out with the time the app made it, not the time the person clocked on Blip, so InVentry's times can be some minutes late. Use BrightHR, never InVentry, for hours and pay. The app never changes BrightHR.
