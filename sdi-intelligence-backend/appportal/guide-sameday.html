SDI Live

A simple guide for account managers and designers. Talk or type the client's brief to Sid (or Beth, Ray or Isla). They ask what's missing, draw the concept when you ask, price it, make the client pack, and make the SDI brief PDF. Updated 21 November 2026.

On your phone, from anywhere. Open SDI Live with the button above. It signs you in with your SDI account (no team code), and everything works, Talk live included. Add it to your home screen from the browser's menu and it opens like an app.

On the office address (10.0.0.5, plain http) nobody is signed in, and the browser won't give it the microphone. So it takes you to the same page on the secure address, where Microsoft 365 says who you are and Talk live works. Typed briefs, client files, renders, the GA, the client sheet and the brief PDF work everywhere.

Past work. SDI Live reads SDI's past jobs from W: and K: (more than 12,000 of them, with their pictures), so it can say what SDI has made before, what it cost, and show work that looks like your brief.

Start

  1. Open SDI Live. It opens in your own name, and your chats are saved there.
  2. Click + New chat.
  3. Choose how you want to brief:
    • Talk live (yellow button): a hands-free voice call. Best straight after a client meeting.
    • Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter.
    • Hold the mic 🎙: hold it, speak, then let go.
  4. Drop any client files on the chat (product photos, artwork, logos, specs). They are read and used for the render.

How a live call goes

  1. "What's the client after?" Say the client and what they want, for example "Milwaukee want a few gondola ends."
  2. "Which pack do you need?" Client pack (a 3D render, its GA and a one-page client sheet), the internal SDI brief, or both. Carry on talking and it takes it as both.
  3. The brief questions, one at a time, from Andy's template. Answer in any order, change your mind any time - the newest answer wins - and add anything that has no question: it's all kept. Nothing is drawn yet.
  4. You decide when to see it. Say "that's enough detail, crack on and make me a visual" whenever you like - after five questions or thirty. When the questions run out it offers once: "Shall I make the visual now?"
  5. "A past SDI job to follow, or something new?" Name it any way you like: a number, a name, "the latest bunker we did". It always checks first: "Do you mean 12148 - Rollershutter Forecourt Bunker?" Say "can I see those?" to see past work on screen.
  6. The render is drawn (about a minute). To cancel, press ■ Stop or say "stop".
  7. "Any changes, or shall I finalise this one?"
    • Say what to change - "make the header bigger and the shelves white" - and you get a new option (B, C…). Or add details and say "show me".
    • Say "finalise" or "that's the one": the GA, a 3D model to turn round, and the client sheet are made.
  8. "Anything else for the brief?" Then the brief PDF. If questions are still open it asks once, "Are you sure that's everything?", and lists them as open points. Everything is saved in the chat, under Sheets & PDFs.

Pricing it (internal only)

All three estimates are for you, never for the client, and never go on the client sheet. Each one is SDI's cost to make it, per unit, before margin - not a selling price.

You always get one. When you choose a design ("Love it - make the client pack", or "option B is the one"), its estimate is worked out straight away, from SDI's past jobs. If SDI has no past job close enough, the AI works it out instead, which takes about a minute. It appears under the client pack, and in a call Sid tells you the figure.

Want a different one, or a price before you choose? Under every design there are three buttons: Past jobs, AI estimate and Estimate Lite. Each prices the design it sits under. Or just say it:

Voice notes

  • Tap the mic in the chat to record a voice note, like on WhatsApp. Tap again to send. Slide up to lock it for long notes.
  • The reply comes back in your character's voice. Tap Show text under it if you want to read it.

Tips for good results

  • You lead. It never hurries you to finish, and never draws until you ask.
  • Short answers are fine: "yes", "no", "both", "six". Say them clearly at normal volume.
  • Say numbers you're sure of. SDI Live never invents sizes. If it didn't catch a number, it asks again.
  • Name the brand. Product brands like No7, Elvive or Lindor go on the header.
  • Upload the real logo or products if you have them. The render uses them first.

If it didn't hear you

  • If it says "Didn't quite catch that", say the answer again, a bit slower, in a full short sentence.
  • In a noisy room, hold the 🎙 button while you speak and let go when you finish. Then it only listens to you.
  • Can't hear it? Press Can't hear it? Sound check on the call bar: it plays a tone and shows your mic level.
  • Need a minute? Press Pause. To stop, press ✕, or say "goodbye".
  • If a banner says why the voice can't start (microphone blocked, no connection, account out of credit), it means exactly that - typing still works.
  • Still stuck? Type the answer in the box. Typing and talking go into the same brief.
