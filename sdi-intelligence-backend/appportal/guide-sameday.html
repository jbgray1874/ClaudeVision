SDI Same Day

A simple guide for account managers and designers. Talk or type the client's brief. Same Day asks what's missing, draws the concept, makes the client pack, and makes the SDI brief PDF.

In the office or on the VPN. Open SameDay with the button above (10.0.0.5:5020) and enter the team access code — ask Muhammad Yazir for it.

Type for now. Browsers only allow the microphone on secure (https) pages, and the office address is not one yet, so Talk live, Hold the mic and voice notes come later, with SameDay's secure address. Typed briefs, client files, renders, the GA, the client sheet and the brief PDF all work today.

If it can't find a past SDI job, that is expected for now: past work lives on W:, and the server cannot read it yet.

Start

  1. Open SDI Same Day and pick your name: Andy, James or Muhammad. Your chats are saved under your name.
  2. Click + New chat.
  3. Choose how you want to brief:
    • Talk live (yellow button): a hands-free voice call. Best straight after a client meeting.
    • Type in the box at the bottom and press Enter.
    • Hold the mic 🎙: hold it, speak, then let go.
  4. Drop any client files on the chat (product photos, artwork, logos, specs). They are read and used for the render.

How a live call goes

  1. "What did the client ask for?" Say the client and what they want, for example "Milwaukee want a few gondola ends." The client's logo and brand colours appear on screen, and the chat turns to their colours.
  2. "Which pack do you need?" Answer: client pack, internal pack, or both.
    • Client pack: a 3D render, its GA drawing and a one-page client sheet.
    • Internal pack: the full SDI brief PDF for the studio.
  3. A few quick render questions: what the unit is, its size, the products, and the colours. That's all. If you don't know an answer, say "you choose" or "just assume". Anything else is assumed, marked "(assumed)", and you change it on the picture.
  4. "Is there a past SDI job or file I should follow, or shall I make something new?" Name it any way you like: a job number, a name, a fixture, a file name, or "the latest bunker we did". Same Day finds it on W: and always checks first: "Do you mean 12148 - Rollershutter Forecourt Bunker?" Say yes, or no to see the other matches. The render and GA then follow that job closely. Say "show me what we've done" to see past work on screen.
  5. The render is drawn (about 40–60 seconds). The mic pauses while it works and opens again when the picture is on screen. To cancel, press ■ Stop on the stage or say "stop".
  6. "Do you like it, or any changes?"
    • To change it, say what to change, for example "make the header bigger and the shelves white". You get a new option (B, C…).
    • To keep it, say "I like it" or "finalise". Same Day makes the GA and the client sheet: Client pack ready.
  7. "Now let's start the internal brief." Answer the internal questions: project name, account manager, deadline, budget and so on.
  8. "Anything else to add?" Then the brief PDF is made. Everything is saved in the chat, under Sheets & PDFs.

Voice notes

  • Tap the mic in the chat to record a voice note, like on WhatsApp. Tap again to send. Slide up to lock it for long notes.
  • The reply comes back as a voice note too. Tap Show text under it if you want to read it.

Tips for good results

  • Short answers are fine: "yes", "no", "both", "six". Say them clearly at normal volume.
  • Say numbers you're sure of. Same Day never invents sizes. If it didn't catch a number, it asks again.
  • Wait for the question to finish before you answer. Talking over it can cut the question off.
  • Name the brand. Product brands like No7, Elvive or Lindor go on the header.
  • Upload the real logo or products if you have them. The render uses them first.

If it didn't hear you

  • If it says "Didn't quite catch that", say the answer again, a bit slower, in a full short sentence: "No, it doesn't need lighting."
  • In a noisy room, hold the 🎙 button while you speak and let go when you finish. Then it only listens to you.
  • Need a minute? Press Pause. Press Continue and it repeats the last question.
  • To jump to the next question, press ⏭. To stop, press ✕, or say "that's all".
  • Still stuck? Type the answer in the box. Typing and talking go into the same brief.
