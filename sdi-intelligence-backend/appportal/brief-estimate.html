Brief to Book

Describe the product the way you would tell an estimator, press Run, and the estimating engine reads the brief, sights the parts and files a provisional costing workbook for the estimators to look over. Nothing goes to a customer from here: the book is marked provisional and every size and count the brief did not fix is listed for confirmation.

The enquiry

The new enquiry's own number. Not a past job's: the run files under this number and clears its working folder.

What we know (optional)

Every fact here replaces a guess in the costing. Leave a box empty and the book lists it as an assumption to confirm.

What this is, and is not

A brief is read by the vision model as stated facts and the ordinary pricing waterfall prices what it sights. It is a costing draft for an estimator, not a quotation; the customer quote is withheld until an estimator releases it on the estimating page.
