TTI Despatch

1. What it is

Customer Services plan what leaves SDI for TTI in the TTI Despatch Plan, a workbook with a sheet per week. This app reads every weekly sheet at once and answers the questions people ask of it every day: what is leaving on a day, where an order is, what is blocked, what was never updated, and what is wrong in the sheet itself. It also finds files in the TTi Design, Production and Prototype folders.

It only reads

Nothing here changes the workbook, books anything in, moves anything to Completed Orders, changes or moves files, or sends email. Your spreadsheet stays exactly as it is and you keep using it as now; this is a second way of looking at it. The morning summary is written for you to copy and send yourself.

Every answer says which sheet and row it came from, such as Oct 26 WK 40!row 5, so you can check it at source.

2. Getting started

  1. Open the app portal at sdiintelligence-sdidisplays.msappproxy.net/app on a phone or laptop, in or out of the office.
  2. Sign in with your usual SDI Microsoft account.
  3. Only need the drawings? …/api/tti/despatch#files opens straight on Find TTi files.
  4. On the TTI Despatch square, tap Open TTI Despatch →. Or go straight to sdiintelligence-sdidisplays.msappproxy.net/api/tti/despatch. You can bookmark it, or add it to your phone's home screen.

Who can use it

Named people only, because the plan is a live commercial tracker and every look at it is recorded against the person. If you see The TTI despatch view is for named Customer Services people, ask James Gray to add you.

The plan is read as you, so you also need your normal access to the Despatch Plan on SharePoint. The file search (section 7) does not need that.

3. Where the answers come from

  • The TTI Despatch Plan on SharePoint (Sue's site, TTI Delivery Schedule), read fresh each time you ask, with your own Microsoft sign-in.
  • Every weekly sheet that has the columns SDI ORDER NO, PART CODE and LORRY FROM SDI in its first 20 rows. Other sheets are ignored.
  • As current as the last save. The line at the bottom of each answer says when the workbook was last saved. An edit someone has not saved yet is not in the answer.
  • Dates are worked out against the day the plan was saved, not today, so an answer always matches the workbook it was read from.

4. The tabs, one by one

Leaving on a day

Tap Today, Tomorrow or This week, or pick a date and tap What is leaving that day?. It lists the lines whose LORRY FROM SDI date is that day. That's when a lorry is planned to leave SDI, not the customer's due date.

  • Tomorrow is the next working day: on a Friday the button says Monday.
  • This week shows Monday to Friday, a heading per day, with "nothing planned" on a day with no lorries. A Saturday or Sunday departure is shown too, if there is one. At the weekend the button says Next week.
  • Lines with a date it cannot read are shown in a foldaway. If nothing else is found, it says those cannot be ruled out, so check them in the workbook before relying on "nothing is leaving".
  • Lines with no departure date yet (blank, N/A, TBC) are listed separately; that is normal for work not yet planned.
  • Each line shows its trailer column (BOOKED, a registration) and a green loaded tag once despatch have written "loaded" there.
  • Pallets are added up for the day, and for each day of the week: the PALLETS column of those lines. Several lines often go on one lorry, so it is a number of pallets, not lorries. A pallet count written as text, or left blank, is not counted, and the note under the list says which lines those are.
  • A plan is not proof that goods have left: that is the delivery note, the invoice number and "loaded" in the trailer column.

Search all weeks

Type an SDI order number, customer PO, part code or TTI account and press Enter. It searches every weekly sheet at once.

  • Matches are lines where those columns contain every word you typed (part of a number is enough).
  • Mentions are other lines that contain the words anywhere, usually in the comments.
  • Up to 60 of each are shown, soonest departure first.

Chase list

What is blocked, and by what. Set how many days ahead to chase paperwork (7 by default, 0 to 90). A line can appear under more than one heading.

Morning summary

Pick a day and tap Build the summary. It writes a short summary you can read, or Copy as text and paste into an email. Nothing is sent. For the day chosen it lists:

  • Due out, each with anything still missing: not booked in with TTI, labels and batch not marked done, no customer PO, no pallet count, no delivery address or destination;
  • Planned to have left, not invoiced: earlier departures with no invoice number;
  • any lines whose date could not be read.

Data quality

Tap Check the workbook to list the cells that stop answers being exact, so they can be fixed at source:

  • Departure dates that cannot be read;
  • Customer due dates that cannot be read;
  • Pallet counts stored as text;
  • Quantities stored as text;
  • Order lines with no order number;
  • The same order and part on more than one row (it may be a genuine split delivery);
  • Month spellings it corrected and used, such as FRI 6TH NIV TBC read as November. Fix the cell rather than relying on this.

Project tracker

Liam's Milwaukee Amsterdam Conference 2027 tracker (PC 402426), read from the TTi Projects SharePoint site as you, every time you open the tab. Each sheet is shown row by row, with every column as it is in the tracker, and where it came from (file › sheet › row).

  • Type in the box to keep only the rows that have all the words you type: a name, a part, a status, a date.
  • A W:\ drawing path in a row gets an Open button; a folder path gets Find in TTi files.
  • Open it in SharePoint opens the tracker itself, to change something.
  • If your account cannot open the tracker in SharePoint, the tab says so; ask Liam to share it.

Find TTi files

Search the TTi folders on W: by file or folder name. See section 7.

Done, not needed, or still to do

As Sue uses the sheet: N/A means that step is not needed for the job, so it is never chased. TBC means waiting on information, so it is still to do, as are a blank cell, NEEDED, Need to Relabel, AWAIT PO and similar. Anything else, such as a date, YES, PROOF DONE or a number, counts as done.

6. How dates are read

  • A real Excel date is used as it is.
  • Typed dates are read UK way round: 23/10/2026, 23.10.26, 2026-10-23, or words such as FRI 23RD OCT.
  • No year written: the year is chosen so the date falls near when the plan was saved, and the weekday, if given, must agree.
  • TBC, await, provisional or estimated in the cell: read, but marked tentative.
  • Blank, N/A, TBC on its own and similar: "no date yet", which is normal, not an error.
  • Two dates in one cell, or words like "moved", "from", "until": not guessed. Listed as unreadable.
  • A weekday that does not match the date: not guessed. Listed as unreadable.
  • A month misspelt by one letter (NIV for Nov) is corrected only when a weekday confirms it, and is listed in Data quality.

7. Finding TTi files

The Find TTi files tab, and the same question in ChatGPT, search by file and folder name in:

  • Design: W:\Design\Customers April 08 Onwards\TTi
  • Production: W:\Production\TTi
  • Prototype: W:\Prototype\TTi
  1. Type a job number, drawing number, part code or product name, or several words. Every word must appear in the file's name or folder path.
  2. Optionally choose one folder, and a kind: drawings (PDF, DXF, DWG), 3D models (SolidWorks, STEP), images, documents, or folders only.
  3. Results show the name, the full W:\ path, the folder, size and date modified. Best name matches come first, then the most recently changed.
  4. Open shows a PDF or picture in a new tab, on a phone too, in or out of the office. Other files (DWG, SolidWorks, Excel) download. Copy path is for opening it from the W: drive in the office instead.

Good to know

It searches names, not what is inside files. It works from an index rebuilt on a schedule, and the line at the bottom says when; a file saved since then will not be found yet. Only files in the three TTi folders open, and nothing here changes, moves or deletes a file.

8. Using it in ChatGPT

The same questions can be asked in ChatGPT, typed or by voice, through the SDI TTI Despatch connector. ChatGPT reads the plan as you, with your Microsoft sign-in, and only reads.

Connecting, once

  1. In ChatGPT, open Plugins: the @ icon in the left-hand strip, or the Plugins button under the message box. Choose SDI TTI Despatch (on the Wearesdi tab). If you cannot see it, ask James.
  2. Tap Connect, then Continue to SDI TTI Despatch. A page asks you to confirm that ChatGPT may look up the TTI Despatch Plan and search the TTi folders as you, read-only. Tap Continue.
  3. Choose your SDI Microsoft account and approve the sign-in (MFA).
  4. If ChatGPT offers Always allow, it is safe: every tool only reads.
