User guide

SDI Intelligence AM CRM

Your account tracker, asked questions and updated by speaking — at your desk, on your phone, or hands-free in the car. Every change is read back to you and saved only when you say yes.

1. What it does

It reads your account and project tracker — the workbook you already live in — and answers questions about it out loud. It can also change a cell for you, but only after reading the change back and hearing you agree.

  • Only your records. It filters the tracker to the rows where you are the owner. Other people's rows are not shown and cannot be changed.
  • It reads the workbook as you. Your own Microsoft sign-in, your own access. Nobody inherits the ability to see it from the app.
  • One cell at a time. A change writes a single cell. The workbook is never replaced or re-uploaded, so nothing outside the cell you named can move.
  • Nothing is saved without a yes. Every change is proposed, read back, and only written when you confirm it.

2. Getting started

  1. On your phone or laptop, open sdiintelligence-sdidisplays.msappproxy.net/app. It works over mobile data or any Wi-Fi; no VPN is needed.
  2. Sign in with your usual SDI Microsoft account, not an admin account.
  3. Tap Open SDI Intelligence AM CRM →.
  4. On a phone, choose Add to Home Screen from the browser menu, so it opens like an app and the microphone keeps its permission.

The first time you use the microphone the browser asks permission. Say yes. On an iPhone it may ask again the first time in each new session — that is the phone, not the app.

Who can use it

Asking questions is open to anyone who can see the tracker. Changing anything is limited to named people. If you are not one of them you will be told so plainly, and you can still ask questions.

3. Asking it things

Tap Tap to talk and speak normally. It listens until you pause for about three seconds, then works out what you said and answers out loud. Tap the microphone again to interrupt it at any point.

If you would rather type, use the box and tap Send. The microphone switches off while you type and you will see Mic off while you type.

Things worth asking:

  • "What's due this week?"
  • "What's overdue?"
  • "Tell me about Barbour."
  • "What's my biggest job by value?"
  • "What am I waiting on?"

Say the name however it sounds

Speech recognition mangles client names, so it matches by sound as well as spelling — "barber" finds Barbour, "the perfume shop" finds TPS. If it cannot match a name at all it shows you everything rather than claiming there is nothing.

Dates are read back in words — "the fourteenth of November" rather than 11/14/2026 — because a date read as numbers is easy to mishear.

4. Changing something

Say the change in a normal sentence. You can make several in one go:

"Set Barbour's status to live and the next step to chase the PO, due Friday."

Then three things happen, always in this order:

  1. It reads the change back. For example: Two changes. Barbour: Status from Quoted to Live. Barbour: NEXT STEPS from Awaiting sign-off to Chase the PO. Shall I save them?
  2. You answer. Say yes, or tap Yes — write it. To abandon it, say no or tap No — cancel.
  3. It saves, and tells you what it saved — Done — I updated …

Only a plain yes saves

"Yes", "yep", "go ahead", "save it" all save. Anything with a doubt in it does not: "yes but change the date too" cancels and starts again, on purpose. If it cannot tell, it says I need a clear yes or no — or use the buttons rather than guessing.

If somebody else edits the row while you are talking about it, the save is abandoned and you are told: Someone changed that record while we were talking. Nothing was written. Ask for it again and re-propose. This is deliberate — it is what stops your change quietly overwriting theirs.

Amounts go in as plain numbers. Say "fifteen thousand five hundred"; it writes 15500. If it cannot read an amount clearly it refuses rather than guessing at it.

To add to a cell rather than replace it, say so: "add 'spoke to Dave' to the next steps". It sends the old text plus the new words.

5. Drive mode

Tap 🚗 Drive for hands-free use. The screen becomes large and simple, the microphone reopens by itself after each answer, and you confirm changes by saying yes or no rather than tapping. Tap Exit to come out of it.

Driving

Set it going before you move off, and treat it like a phone call: if it needs your eyes, it needs you parked.

6. Your morning briefing

▶ Listen to my briefing reads out what is due, what has slipped and what is worth chasing. Show today's review shows the same thing written down, and Email it now sends it to you.

It also arrives by email on weekday mornings without being asked.

7. Recent activity

Show recent activity lists what has been proposed, saved, cancelled or refused lately, newest first — whether it came from this app or from ChatGPT. Every change is recorded with who made it and when, so there is always a record of what the app did.

8. What it will not do

  • Change somebody else's records. It only ever sees rows where you are the owner.
  • Change the owner column. That would hand a record away, so it is refused.
  • Type over a calculated cell. If a column holds a formula — margin, for instance — it refuses rather than silently replacing the calculation with a fixed number. If it cannot tell whether a cell is calculated, it still refuses: better to make you do it by hand than flatten a formula nobody notices for a month.
  • Replace the workbook. It writes one cell through Excel's own interface and never uploads a file over the top of yours.
  • Save anything you have not agreed to.

If it says it saved, it saved

In October a change that had been written was reported back as a failure, because the app checked the cell against a copy of the workbook that had not caught up yet. That is fixed: the write and the check now happen in one conversation with Excel, so they cannot disagree. If you are ever unsure, open the tracker and look — and tell James either way.

10. Help

Questions, something that looks wrong, or a change you are not sure went in: contact James Gray, AI and Systems Controller. If a change did not behave as expected, say roughly when it was — there is a record of every proposal and every save, and the time is enough to find it.

This is a sandbox pilot running against a copy of the tracker, not the live one.
