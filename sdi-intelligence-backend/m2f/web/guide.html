Using Monday2Friday

Monday2Friday is the account and delivery workspace in SDI Intelligence. During the pilot it reads the account tracker, so the figures are the tracker's figures and you keep updating the tracker as normal. What you add here (freezes, reasons, variations, answers) is saved in Monday2Friday.

Getting in

  1. On the SDI network, open the SDI Intelligence portal and click Monday2Friday, or go straight to sdi-intelligence.sdi.local:8071/m2f.
  2. Sign in with your SDI Microsoft account if asked. Freezes, notes, variations and answers are recorded under your name automatically.
  3. The As of month at the top sets "this month" for every screen. It starts at the current month; change it to look back or ahead.

Status colours

Invoiced · Confirmed · TBC · Risk · Unclassified

These come from the colour of the cell in the tracker and the legend at the top of each tab. Unclassified means the cell's colour isn't on that tab's legend, which is one of the questions for Kayleigh. Once she confirms the mapping, Confirmed shows as PO received and TBC as Awaiting PO.

Your week

Monday, Kayleigh: open Weekly review and freeze the forecast (for example "Forecast freeze 1 Dec"). That's the baseline for the week.
During the week, AMs and PMs: keep the tracker up to date as now. Log any change to agreed scope in Variations as it comes up.
Before the review, AMs: open your AM workspace. Anything that moved on your projects since the freeze is listed; add the reason and next action on Weekly review.
Review, Kayleigh: Weekly review shows the change in this month, next month and the year, each movement with its reason and owner, and open variations. Needs a decision on the dashboard shows what's at risk.

Weekly review in detail

  • Freeze forecast now takes a copy of every project's monthly forecast and status. Give it a name you'll recognise. You can freeze as often as you like; pick which freeze to compare with from the list.
  • Each movement is described in plain words, for example "£20,000 moved from Mar 2027 to Apr 2027", "£500 added in May 2027" or "Mar 2027 TBC to Confirmed".
  • Type the reason, the next action and the owner, then Save on that row. They stay attached to that freeze.
  • A project deleted from the tracker after the freeze still shows, marked "no longer in the tracker", so nothing disappears silently.

Variations

Log every change to an agreed scope: additional artwork, extra install nights, an extra prototype. Pick the project from the list (start typing its name) so the variation is linked to it and shows on the project's panel. Fill in what you know; sell, margin and PO can be added later with Edit.

Questions for Kayleigh

Before the tracker moves fully into the system, a few things need an owner's decision: what the colours mean, which hidden rows are still live, who owns each account, which budget is right, and some values that sit outside the tracker's own totals. Each question shows what the tracker contains so it can be answered without opening the spreadsheet. Pick an option where there is one, add any notes, and save. The badge in the menu counts the questions still open.

Settings and data

  • Tracker: which file Monday2Friday is reading and when it was last saved. The tracker on the shared drive is re-read automatically whenever it's saved. To test a copy, choose it and click Load this tracker.
  • Does it match the tracker? Each monthly total on the tracker's Forcasting Total sheet next to Monday2Friday's figure. "Matches" means to the penny; any difference is explained in the notes.
  • Account owners: type the AM for each account. A Boots division inherits the Boots owner unless you set its own. This is what drives the AM workspace.
  • People: join different spellings of the same person (for example "KT" and a full name), and set each person's deputy for holiday cover.

Testing the pilot

  1. Check the dashboard's this-month figure against the tracker's Forcasting Total for the same month.
  2. Open three projects you know well and check the forecast month by month and the status colours.
  3. AMs: once owners are set, check your workspace lists the projects you'd expect, and nothing you wouldn't.
  4. PMs: check your overview lists your live jobs.
  5. Freeze the forecast, change a figure in a copy of the tracker, load it, and check Weekly review describes the change correctly.
  6. Log a real variation from this week.
  7. Tell James what's wrong, missing or in the wrong place, with the screen and the project name.

Questions and fixes

A number doesn't match the tracker

Check Settings and data first. The dashboard includes rows hidden in the tracker, as the tracker's totals do. Amounts typed as text (for example "£500.00" typed with the pound sign) are counted by Monday2Friday but ignored by Excel's totals, and are listed under Questions for Kayleigh.

My changes in the tracker aren't showing

Save the workbook. Monday2Friday re-reads it the next time a screen loads after the save. If someone is testing with an uploaded copy, the newer of the two files is used.

The AM workspace is empty

Account owners haven't been set yet. They're set in Settings and data once Kayleigh has confirmed who owns what.

It says my sign-in has expired

Sessions last a working day. Click Sign in and you'll come back to the screen you were on.

What isn't here yet

Project CPA plans, Debbie's Finance queue, team capacity, QBR packs, holiday cover, the morning WhatsApp brief and meeting follow-up. Coming next in the menu lists what each one is waiting for.
