Running themes across the programme

Four threads run through every review this cycle, and all four are now standing behaviour of the model:

1 · The shop's own figures win. Department times and rates supplied by the estimators and departments replace engine averages, permanently and for every comparable job.

2 · No silent guesses. An item without a confirmed source stays visibly unpriced and asks for a person — never a closest-match presumption, never a carried-forward historic figure.

3 · Everything says where it came from. Every line names the system, person or drawing that decided it, so a review is a check rather than an investigation.

4 · Rules, not patches. Each correction lands in the engine, is recorded in the change register with the estimator's name against it, and is pinned by an automated test — so the same question is never asked twice.

The register's own trend is the story worth reading twice: phase 1 sends (May–August) mostly went unanswered; the September wave has a 6-of-9 review rate with turnarounds as fast as 19 minutes. The estimating team is engaged, and the review loop is working.
