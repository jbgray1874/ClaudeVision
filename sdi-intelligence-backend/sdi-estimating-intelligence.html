    Parity report — for a job already estimated

    Compare a job the engine has already estimated against the manual estimate for the same job. Both sides have to be named here because both already exist; either can come from the share or be uploaded. Nothing is re-run and no drawings are needed, so this works for jobs estimated months ago.
