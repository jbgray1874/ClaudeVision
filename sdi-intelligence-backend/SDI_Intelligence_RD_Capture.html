The deadline that can lose the claim Critical

Advance notification — a hard gate for first-time claimants

If SDI has not made an R&D claim in the previous three years, HMRC requires an advance notification to be submitted within 6 months of the end of the accounting period in which the R&D took place. Miss this window and the claim is lost entirely — regardless of how strong the underlying R&D is.

Action for Charlotte / specialist, before anything else: confirm SDI's accounting period end date, confirm whether a claim has been made in the last 3 years, and if not, diarise the 6-month notification deadline immediately. This is the single highest-priority item on this page.

The opportunity

There are two distinct mechanisms, and — importantly — under the current rules SDI can pursue both at once.

1 · R&D Tax Relief Merged scheme

For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, the old SME and RDEC schemes merged into a single scheme. It gives a 20% above-the-line credit on qualifying spend — a net benefit of ~15% after corporation tax, or roughly £15,000 back for every £100,000 of qualifying R&D.

Loss-making R&D-intensive SMEs (≥30% of total spend on R&D) may instead qualify for ERIS at an effective rate up to ~27%.

2 · Grant funding No longer cancels relief

Under the merged scheme, the old "subsidised expenditure" rule was dropped. A grant covering part of an R&D project no longer reduces the tax relief available. Grants and tax relief now stack — they no longer cancel each other out.

That changes the calculus entirely: pursuing an Innovate UK or Made Smarter grant is now additive to, not a trade-off against, the tax claim.

What qualifies at SDI

HMRC's test: a project seeking an advance in science or technology by resolving technological uncertainty that a competent professional could not readily deduce. Routine work, aesthetic design and configuration do not qualify. SDI's AI programme sits squarely inside the definition — the following are the qualifying workstreams.

WorkstreamThe technological uncertaintyThe advance sought
AI Estimating Engine Could cost-driving geometry, materials and BOMs be reliably extracted from inconsistent SolidWorks PDF/DXF drawing packs, and a defensible cost produced where the method was not readily deducible? A deterministic engine that reads drawings and produces a costed estimate with a credibility score, failing loud rather than guessing.
Drawing Intelligence / SolidWorks API Can missing flat-pattern DXFs be generated automatically from a GA or PDF, and can drawings be ingested directly from SolidWorks via API? Automated DXF generation to remove the largest single accuracy limiter in the estimating engine.
RAG Pricing Pipeline Could historical quote data be retrieved and re-ranked to price parts the engine has never seen, with quantified confidence? A multi-tier retrieval pipeline with token-overlap re-ranking and per-part confidence flags.
HR / Systems Integration Could disparate systems (BrightHR, InVentry, Blip) be reconciled where no common identifier or department field is exposed by the APIs? A reconciliation pipeline resolving identity across systems with no shared key.
Immersive Design & Visualisation Can photoreal client concepts be generated and iterated from brief using AI and real-time 3D, at a fidelity and speed not previously achievable? An AI-assisted concept pipeline (Unreal Engine + generative tools) for brand-experience work.
ERP / Sage X3 Migration Bespoke integration and data-migration challenges where off-the-shelf approaches do not resolve the technical problem. To be scoped — qualifying elements identified case by case during the programme.

Important caveat

This page structures the technical narrative and evidence — it is not tax advice. The merged-scheme rules, eligibility boundaries, rates, the PAYE/NIC cap and claim mechanics carry real nuance, and HMRC scrutiny of R&D claims has increased markedly. Tax treatment, what qualifies, and how to claim must be confirmed by a qualified R&D tax specialist — which is exactly why the MD asked Charlotte to take advice now.

The value of capturing as we go is that when the specialist and HMRC need the technical justification — the uncertainty, the advance, the work, the time and the cost — it already exists, contemporaneously recorded, rather than reconstructed a year later.
